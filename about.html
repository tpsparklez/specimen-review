About

The Specimen Review is a poetry publication dedicated to STEM disciplines and bridging the gap between humantiies and life sciences. All molecules are welcome

The name Specimen reflects our belief that the objectivity of a specimen can be transfigured into poetry. Whether in paleontology, organic chemistry, or agriculture, poetry becomes both a bridge of knowledge and an intimate lesson for all.

Our Mission

We aim to create a space for poems of all natures (literally). We welcome the voices of scientists, creatives, and those whose poetic niches are underrepresented in contemporary media. The Specimen Review welcomes both emerging and established voices. Please have a look at our submission categories.
