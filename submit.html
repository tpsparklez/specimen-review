Submissions

The Specimen Review invites poets to submit work that attends closely to their targeted discipline for chance to be selected for publication.

Guidelines

  • Please list your discipline/ category you would like to be reviewed under
  • Optional brief third-person bio (50 words max)
  • Simultaneous submissions are welcome

Reading Period

We read submissions on a rolling basis. Responses are generally sent within 1-2 weeks.

Submit Your Work

Please use the form below to submit your poems (PDF or Word). All submissions are saved securely via Google Forms.

Rights

Upon publication, The Specimen Review requests first publication rights. All rights revert to the author upon publication.
